These terms cover the software, not the work. They govern use of the Renew Home Exteriors estimator application and its electronic signing pages. The agreement for your roofing, siding, window or door project is the contract you sign — including its own terms and conditions and warranty. Nothing on this page changes that contract, and where the two differ, the signed contract governs.

Eligibility and age

You must be at least 18 years of age to use the application or to sign a document through it. By accessing either, you represent and warrant that you are 18 or older and have the legal capacity to enter into a binding agreement.

Access by anyone under the age of 13 is expressly prohibited. The application is not directed to children and we do not knowingly permit its use by, or collect information from, anyone under 13. The Privacy Policy sets out how we handle a report of such use.

This is not a formality. A contract for exterior work binds the property owner, and an agreement signed by a minor may be voidable — an outcome that serves neither party once work has begun. Our project managers confirm that the person signing is an owner of the property or an authorized decision maker for it, and we may decline or void a signature obtained otherwise.

If we learn that a user is under 18 we will terminate their access. If we learn that a user is under 13 we will terminate access and delete their information.

The application

The estimator is an internal tool used by Renew Home Exteriors staff to price projects, produce contracts and send them for signature. Access requires an account we issue. Accounts are for named individuals and are not to be shared.

If you are a member of staff: use the application only for company business, only for customers you are working with, and do not attempt to reach records outside the role you have been granted.

Signing pages

Customers reach a signing page through a single-use link we email. Treat that link as confidential — anyone holding it can view the document. Links expire 30 days after they are sent.

Electronic signatures

By drawing or typing a signature and submitting the document, you agree to use electronic records and signatures for that transaction, as provided by the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act and the federal Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act. Your electronic signature is legally binding and equivalent to a handwritten one.

To evidence the signature we record your signature and initials, the legal name you confirm, your email address, your IP address and browser, and the date and time of viewing and signing. The Privacy Policy describes this in full.

You are not required to sign electronically. Tell your project manager if you would prefer to sign on paper and we will arrange it. You may also request a copy of anything you have signed at any time.

Accuracy of estimates

An estimate produced by this application is a proposal, not a completed agreement. Measurements taken from aerial or satellite imagery are approximate and are confirmed by a final measure. Pricing, availability and timelines are subject to the terms of the signed contract.

Availability

We aim to keep the application and the signing pages available, but we do not guarantee uninterrupted service. Maintenance, provider outages and connectivity problems can interrupt access. If a signing link will not load, contact us and we will reissue it or complete the signing another way.
