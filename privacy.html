Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

What this policy covers. This policy applies to the Renew Home Exteriors pricing estimator application and the electronic document signing pages used to execute contracts. It does not cover the Renew Home Exteriors public website, which is operated separately.

Who we are

Renew Home Exteriors, 1361 Wooster Rd W, Unit A, Barberton, OH 44203. Telephone (330) 208-9366. Email RHEOhio@gmail.com. We are the controller of the information described below.

Information we collect

From our staff

Staff sign in with a Google account. We receive the account's name, email address and Google account identifier. We do not receive or store Google passwords. We record a role for each user, which determines what they can see and do.

From customers

When one of our project managers prepares an estimate for you, we record the information needed to price and perform the work:

  • Name, property address, email address and telephone number
  • Details of the project, measurements, material selections and pricing
  • Photographs of the property taken by our staff during the visit
  • Aerial and satellite imagery and roof or siding measurements obtained for your address
  • Financing options discussed, where applicable

When you sign a document electronically

Signing pages are reached through a single-use link sent to you by email. No account or password is required. To make the signature legally admissible under the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act and the federal E-SIGN Act, we record:

  • Your signature and initials as you draw or type them
  • The full legal name you confirm
  • Your email address
  • Your IP address and browser user-agent string
  • The date and time you viewed and signed, and your consent to sign electronically
  • A complete copy of the signed document as it appeared to you

How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and revise your estimate
  • To produce and execute your contract and any change orders
  • To perform the work and support the warranty
  • To keep the business records the law requires us to keep

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

  • Signing links expire 30 days after they are sent and cannot be used afterwards.
  • Signed contracts, change orders and the associated signature records are retained as business and warranty records.
  • Estimates that are never signed, and photographs attached to them, are retained while the opportunity is active and thereafter as ordinary business records.

Security

All traffic to the application and the signing pages is encrypted in transit. Access to customer records is restricted by role, and enforced by the database itself rather than only by the application. Signing links use a single-use 64-character token. Private documents are stored in non-public storage.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your information we will act promptly and notify you where the law requires it.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to retain it. Records attached to a signed contract generally must be kept. Contact us at RHEOhio@gmail.com or (330) 208-9366.

You are never required to sign electronically. If you would rather sign on paper, tell your project manager and we will arrange it.

Age restrictions and children

The application and the signing pages are intended solely for individuals who are at least 18 years of age. They are not directed to, and may not be used by, anyone under 18. By using either, you represent that you are 18 or older.

Access by anyone under the age of 13 is expressly prohibited, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. Consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, if we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly and without requiring a request.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, contact us at RHEOhio@gmail.com or (330) 208-9366 and we will delete it. Where we learn that a user is under 18, we will terminate that access and delete information we are not otherwise required to retain.

We do not knowingly market to minors, and we do not use any information collected through the application to build advertising profiles of any user, of any age.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting how we handle information already collected will be communicated directly.
